vTiger CRM Auto Update

Red cells in, clean tickets out.

Upload the sheet you already track changes in — the app finds every red-highlighted cell and pushes just those fields into vTiger, ticket by ticket.

Auto-detected changes

Red fills are found automatically — no manual list of what changed.

Mappings remembered

Match a column to a vTiger field once — it's reused on every upload.

Review before you push

See exactly which tickets and fields will change, and uncheck any of them.

Verified, not just "success"

Each update is re-checked against vTiger so a silently-dropped field gets flagged.

Need access? Ask your admin to add your vTiger username under Settings → Users.

      Every person who should be able to sign in needs an entry here: their vTiger username, their vTiger access key, and their role.
